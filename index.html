How the Plinko board works

The board in PLINKO GAME is arranged like a triangle of pegs. Each time the ball hits a peg, it can move left or right, so the final result depends on many small random turns. The bottom row contains pockets with different multipliers, and the closer a pocket is to the edge, the less likely it is to land there.
